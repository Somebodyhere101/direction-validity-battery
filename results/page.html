Does the Direction Track the Process?

A published direction in a model's hidden states is usually defended by two facts: it separates two sets of prompts, and pushing along it changes the output. This study runs a fixed battery of checks that ask more of it: does it follow what the model actually does on each item, beat a direction built from the same prompts without their labels, and steer beyond random pushes that change the output just as much?

Verdicts so far

One verdict per direction and model, by the preregistered rules (Stage 1 §6). The chips show each check: A tracking, C the label-free nuisance comparison (tracking / steering), D steering at matched output change, E where the effect is handed on. A direction's overall verdict is the one it gets in at least two of its three models.
